## Before a GPU engineering attempt

1. Find a material check-preserving CPU improvement. Both recent Python
   micro-optimizations failed their fixed speed gates.
2. Qualify real birth, exec, stop and actual-reap containment. The short-lived
   unrearmed probe does not establish a 1800-second attempt lifetime.
3. After a credible fix and renewed explicit authority, validate the exact8GiB
   path within unchanged internal29/external30 limits with adequate headroom.
   Existing recovery/full-volume permissions are consumed; retained data stays
   untouched.
4. Finish the native-helper header/API and compiler-process plan before any
   separately approved implementation/build. Compiler descendants cannot be
   assumed contained by an external timeout.
5. Freeze compatible Linux runtime/image, approved inputs, GPU fit, exact
   interpreter/stager authority and complete SDK/stop/cleanup integration.
6. Review gross preparation, attempt and cleanup accounting: $30 total
   ($25 plan + $5 reserve), one attempt, at most1800seconds, no retry. This is not
   a claim of a provider-enforced spending cap.

The final approved attempt must exercise actual SDK ownership, native two-phase
training/restore/update/export, unchanged complete verification and billed-end
cleanup. Those results cannot be claimed from local fixtures.

No defensible same-day GPU ETA. The next work is the native-helper/API/build
containment design, not another unsupported micro-optimization.
